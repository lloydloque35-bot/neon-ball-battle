### The Fate Notebook (in battle)

- A **📖 NOTEBOOK** button appears when you play Fate Note Ball.
- The notebook shows each opponent **in this match**:
  - HP, harmful effects, seal and its time left;
  - death-sentence countdown and how many hits will break it;
  - chain time and resistance timers;
  - when revealed by Shinigami Vision: cooldowns and current weakness.
- It also shows your Fate Energy and the seal history of this match.
- **✎ WRITE** picks the next name to write. It is sent as a battle input, so online and verified battles replay it exactly.
- Nothing outside the current match is ever shown (no accounts, no history, no other players' data).
- The notebook works with touch on phones (big buttons, bottom sheet).

## REALITY COLLISION (The Void Below vs The Absolute Above)

- When both are in a battle on opposite sides:
  - a **⚔ REALITY COLLISION ⚔** announcement plays;
  - the arena splits crimson / gold;
  - both entrances play.
- When their ultimates meet, it becomes a **REALITY CLASH**. A timing bar runs for 2.4 s, and each side presses **once**
  (the **⚔ PRESS!** button, or Enter / C).
- The result is **60% timing + 40% battle state** (ultimate power × attack × HP). Choosing a ball never decides it alone.
- On AUTO, the AI presses for you, unless you press first. On MANUAL, you press yourself.
- The presses are battle inputs, so the server's re-simulation decides the result.
- The winner's ultimate goes through and gets **REALITY DOMINANCE**: +10% damage for 5 s.

## Purchases — how they are kept safe

- **50,000 coins:** one database transaction.
  - It refuses if you already own the ball.
  - It takes the coins only if you have enough, so the balance never goes below 0.
  - It marks the ball owned and records the purchase (one per player and ball, so the database refuses a second one).
  - It writes the audit log.
  - A double tap or a repeated request charges once.
- **Fate Note Ball, PHP 99:** the same verified shop as the other God Balls.
  - **PayMongo:** the server asks PayMongo itself whether the exact amount was paid.
  - **QR:** nothing unlocks until the owner presses Approve, and each reference number works once.
  - Webhooks, "Check" and "Restore purchases" never grant twice.
  - No card numbers, wallet logins, PINs or OTPs ever reach the game.
  - No bank details are in the game files.
- None of the three can come from summon orbs, events, the Archon or an admin event.
- The browser never decides ownership, coins, EXP or mastery. The server does.
